A slide is a few lines of HTML

<section class="slide" data-layout="stats">
  <h2>Small kit, big range</h2>
  <div class="stats">
    <div class="stat">
      <span class="num" data-count="6">6</span>
      <span class="label">themes</span>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

No CSS, no animation code: the layout and the motion come from the engine and the theme.
