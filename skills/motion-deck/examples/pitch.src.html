The problem

Office plants die because nobody owns them.

Every office buys plants, and every office watches them fade. Facilities teams have bigger fires to fight, and employees assume somebody else is watering. The plants are not expensive, but the replacement cycle and the sad corner of dry leaves cost more than anyone budgets. This is a service problem, not a gardening problem.

Why they fail

Three ways offices lose plants

Overwatering

Everyone helps, nobody tracks.

Neglect

Holidays empty the floor.

Wrong spot

Light never matched the species.

We interviewed forty office managers. Three causes came up again and again. Overwatering happens when many people help without a shared record. Neglect happens over holidays and in hybrid weeks. The wrong spot is a purchasing mistake that nobody notices until the leaves turn yellow. Each cause is a data problem we can measure.

The answer

A sensor, an app and a visit

A small sensor sits in each pot and reports moisture and light every hour. A hub per floor collects the readings. The app flags only the plants that need help, so nobody waters by habit. Once a month a local grower visits with exactly the supplies the app predicted. The office does nothing.

The product

One subscription, no chores

  • Sensors and hub included
  • Monthly visit by a local grower
  • Replacements at no extra cost

From €89 a month per floor.

Customers pay one monthly fee per floor. It covers the hardware, the visits and every replacement plant. We keep the hardware, so the customer never buys a sensor. Growers are paid per visit, which lets us start in a new city with a handful of local partners rather than a large payroll.
